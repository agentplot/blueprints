# Three exits

A sounding's session is free inside its place. It reaches beyond the
place through three commands, its exits: fixed outputs the session
gives and sounding acts on (65). The session ends a round with
`sounding conclude`, and may offer and ask along the way.

| Exit | What follows |
|---|---|
| `sounding conclude` | Writes `conclusion.md`: the answer, its evidence, what it would change, the work it proposes, and what stays open. The round ends, and the operator decides the conclusion. |
| `sounding offer` | Hands a finding beyond the question to `millpond land` as a capture of its own (62), one key per offer. The session goes on (58). |
| `sounding ask` | Puts a question only the operator can answer on a card. The session waits for the answer, then goes on. |

## Conclude

`sounding conclude` writes `conclusion.md` and ends the round. A
conclusion holds:

- the answer to the question;
- its evidence;
- what the answer would change;
- the work it proposes;
- what stays open.

The operator decides it ([Deciding a conclusion](deciding.md)). When
the operator accepts, each piece of work the conclusion proposes
becomes an item of its own or an entry in crew's plan, so the
conclusion names each piece apart.

## Offer

A session finds things beyond its question as it works: a defect in a
tool it read, a record gone stale, a second question. A finding about
the question belongs in the conclusion. A finding beyond it belongs in
the inbox, and `sounding offer` hands it to `millpond land` as a
capture of its own (62), as millpond's book has it
([Findings, from a sounding](../millpond/sources.html#findings-from-a-sounding)).

The capture:

- is its own excerpt, so millpond writes its one signal as it lands;
- has the kind `ask`;
- names the sounding as who asserted it;
- has the key `sounding/<name>/<n>`.

Each offer takes its own `<n>`, so two offers are two captures, and
one offer landed twice is still one (111).

sounding prints the offer as contract lines with flywheel-next's
capture crate, and pipes them to `millpond land` where millpond is
installed. The lines pass the conformance pack as any adapter's do
([millpond's contract](../millpond/contract.html)). From there the
finding takes the path every capture takes: curation gives its signal
a move ([millpond's curation](../millpond/curation.html)).

An offer never interrupts the session that gave it (58). The session
goes on with its question.

## Ask

Some things only the operator can answer: a fact only they hold, or a
choice that is theirs to make. `sounding ask` puts such a question on a
card, and the session waits for the answer, then goes on with the
round. A sounding waiting on an answer shows as waiting on the operator
in sounding's herdr plugin ([The operator's surfaces](surfaces.md)).

## Which exit when

| The session has | It uses |
|---|---|
| an answer, or what it knows when the timebox runs out | `sounding conclude` |
| a finding beyond its question | `sounding offer`, and goes on |
| a question only the operator can answer | `sounding ask`, and waits |
