Update Ladder

Every event of a drive arrives in millpond. A policy of plain rules, with no model, gathers the ones it recognises into items over a window, and launches each item as a flume kind at a priority. A new version lands with no agent when its command and the gate pass. Everything the rules don't recognise goes to curation and to you. The same seam links drives: a version published in one drive arrives as a signal in another.

01

The whole system

The runner's pass is the host's clock: it runs millpond's tick, carries every record up to the cloud, and launches what the drive asks. millpond is the junction. The policy sits inside it, between signals and items.

On its ownmillpond launches the item's kind itself (dashed). Items with no kind wait for you in millpond.
With BoomdriveThe item goes up. The drive puts it in your queue, or, when the rule named a kind, has the runner launch it. No decision is raised for a rule's launch.
Either wayThe same rules run, from the same settings file. Only the launcher differs.
02

Three clocks: scan, window, room

Scanning often is cheap: a lookup, no model. Acting is what costs: a launch, a gate run, a landing, a rebuild of every machine, and tokens when an agent steps in. The window separates the two. Four releases over an hour become two launches.

scan · .config/millpond.toml exists
[[adapter]]
name     = "headwater"
command  = "headwater capture"
host     = "mac-studio"
schedule = "*/5 * * * *"   # every 5 minutes
window · .config/millpond.toml new
[[rule]]
name     = "family"
when     = { dependency = "pkg:github/agentplot/*" }
kind     = "bump-set"
priority = "normal"
window   = "*/30 * * * *"  # every 30 minutes
room · flume's config exists
[streams.work]
agents   = 5
priority = 10
pace     = { points = 40, per = "day" }

[streams.work.labels.operation]
owed     = 1   # updates' agents never wait
               # behind construction

Busy development: set the family window to */10 * * * *. Quiet weeks: 0 6 * * *, once a day at six. The scan stays at five minutes either way, so the queue always knows what's waiting.

03

The policy, in full

One file, the inbox's settings, kept by whoever keeps the inbox. Rules are read top to bottom and the first match wins. Every outcome is one of three: launch a kind, wait for you, or drop with a reason.

# Meeting rules: see 05.
name
Names the rule's items: <rule>--<window close>, as point-releases--20261112-1430. The item names its launch, so one window is never launched twice.
when
Facts only: stated by the source, joined from your people list, or read by a reader (05):

A list matches any of its values; a * matches a family by its prefix.
outcome
kind = "update"launch this flume kind when the window closes
kind = falsewait in your queue, at the rule's priority
drop = "reason"millpond's drop move, with its reason
priority
A level from [priority]. It becomes the launch's priority in flume, or the item's place in your queue. millpond carries it and orders nothing itself.
window
Five-field cron, read by weir's cadence like an adapter's schedule, or "now". A release that arrives after an item was sent joins the next one.
04

One item, launched

When the window closes, millpond, or the runner at the drive's ask, makes one launch. The parameters are for the command; the brief is for an agent, if one is ever needed.

the launch flume gains --params-file
flume launch --repository swancloud --kind update \
  --name point-releases--20261112-1430--1 \
  --mark swancloud/point-releases--20261112-1430/1 \
  --priority 10 \
  --params-file item.json \
  --brief-file item.md

The notes of every release between each pin and its new

update@1 · a statechart kind (sketch) new
label          = "operation"   # its share of agents
max_send_backs = 2             # then it waits for you
initial        = "move"

[states.move]            # no agent
command = "run-moves item.json"   # each move, one commit
on      = { ok = "submit", failed = "repair" }

[states.submit]          # sluice's gate
on      = { landed = "done", refused = "repair" }

[states.repair]          # reached only from a failure
agent   = true
brief   = ["failure", "item.md"]
on      = { delivered = "submit" }

fix, flume's own kind, is the same shape without the move state: it starts on the agent. That's the only difference between a patch and a major.

05

One policy over facts, whoever gives them

A meeting has facts as well as words. eddy states its details, millpond joins its people to the list you keep, and a reader writes each signal in a fixed schema. The rules match all three. Curation takes what no rule names, and the judgments that need more than one signal.

statedThe source gives it, as it recorded it: a release's level, a meeting's title, start, length and attendees from eddy's details. No model.
joinedmillpond looks the attendees and speakers up in your people list: name, role, team, and the org their address belongs to. An org marked a client makes client a fact. No model.
readA reader writes it, choosing from closed lists. millpond checks each fact: the excerpt stands in the transcript, the speaker is on it, the values are in their lists. A fact that fails stays in the signal's words, for curation.
one meeting signal, as a rule sees it
{
  "source": "meeting",
  // stated: eddy's details
  "title": "Acme demo prep",
  "start": "2026-11-12T15:00Z",
  "length": "45m",
  "attendees": ["dana@acme.com", "robin@ourco.com",
                "lee@ourco.com"],
  // joined: your people list
  "roles": ["client", "executive", "engineer"],
  "client": "acme",
  "teams": ["web-app"],
  // read: the reader, checked by millpond
  "assertion": "request",
  "said_by": { "name": "Robin Hale", "role": "executive" },
  "about": "web-app",
  "due": "2026-11-14",
  "excerpt": "Can we have the export flow ready
              to show Acme on Friday?",
  "at": "00:12:40"
}

assertion is one of decision, request, action, risk or question. about is one of the drive's teams or repositories. said_by.role is joined from the speaker the reader names, so the model never decides who is an executive.

06

Drives, linked

One seam joins two drives: a version published in one arrives as a signal in another. Teams publish versions up to the executive; the executive publishes strategy and requests down to the teams. The publisher states facts, and each drive's own policy decides what they mean there.

07

Where Boom fits

Boom keeps a drive's claims: what holds. It routes nothing and plans nothing. Its gaps, the places where a claim does not hold, enter millpond like any other event, and the same policy routes them. Claims themselves are never routed; curation reads them to answer or challenge a signal.

10

Calls this design makes

Each is my recommendation. Change any and the rest still hold.

  • The policy lives in the inbox's settings.One file per drive, kept by whoever keeps the inbox, and the same in every setup. Rules reach one repository with repository = "…". A repository's pins stay in its own lock files.
  • Five named priority levels.low, normal, elevated, high, urgent, each mapped to a number in flume's order. Rules name levels, never numbers.
  • Three outcomes: a kind, your queue, or a drop.Every rule ends in exactly one. A drop is millpond's existing drop move, with the rule's reason.
  • Updates carry the operation label.flume already shares a stream's agents by label, so an update's agent stage never waits behind construction work, and the cloud counts updates as Operation, outside the construction gauge.
  • A watching drive always sets its own priority.A publisher's tag is a fact its rules can match; what the publisher thinks is urgent never sets the watcher's priority directly.
  • A meeting's facts come three ways.Stated by eddy's details, joined from your people list, read by a reader. Rules match all three; curation takes what no rule names.
  • A reader picks from closed lists, and millpond checks it.An assertion is one of five; a speaker is one the transcript gives; about is one of the drive's teams or repositories. A fact that fails the check stays in the signal's words.
  • People are a list, never a guess.[[person]] and [[org]] in the inbox's settings, or the drive's members in the cloud. Who is an executive or a client is joined, never read.
  • A rule on stated facts runs before reading.A drop there means no reader is charged, so a stand-up costs no tokens.
  • A cloud-only drive runs the same policy in the cloud.weir's evaluator runs there, and the cloud's clock stands in for the runner's tick.
