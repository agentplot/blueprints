Plans

The loop keeps turning while your laptop sleeps.

The flywheel is an always-on planning loop over your GitHub repositories. It takes in your signals — meetings, chat, pull requests — turns them into a plan you answer one line at a time, and drains the approved work through agent sessions on your machines or in the cloud.

The promise, in one sentence. Nothing of yours is ever in a process or on a disk that serves another organization. Your code exists only on a machine created for your work and destroyed when it ends. Everything we keep between ticks is encrypted at rest under a key that exists for your organization alone, and is unwrapped only while your tick runs.

state lives in your GitHub, in plaintext, always a plan never authorizes; roles do exceeding a limit is one attention line, never a stopped loop

Where your data lives

By tier, machine by machine

Your source code and your state are on GitHub in plaintext, yours, at every tier. What changes as you climb is only what exists on our side, and how little of it there is.

On our side
Nothing at all.
Your code
GitHub and your own disk.

Cloud agent

A queue, a role and a key, one encrypted cache object, a scheduler entry. No pool.

Still only GitHub and your laptop, which keeps building.
tier 2

Pools

You add
An image and a bound.
On our side
Tier 1, plus pool hosts on demand — one organization each, terminated at retire.
Your code
On a machine made for that job, on a disk encrypted under your key, destroyed when it ends.
tier 3

Your account

You add
One IAM role in your AWS account trusting our OIDC issuer for your organization.
On our side
The same dispatcher, assuming your role with a per-tick token. We store no credential.
Your code
Your key, cache, queue and pool image all live in your account. Deleting the role ends our access.
Moving up

Each step is one decision, not a migration

Nothing is re-keyed by hand, and nothing you already have stops working.

1

Free → Hobby

Sign in to the hosted account with the same GitHub identity. The service creates the account, invites the same usernames and carries every address. One cloud agent is added from a preset, and the laptop keeps its place as a host.

2

Hobby → Pro

The pools flag turns on and the pool form appears under Hosts. The first pool declares an image and a bound within the plan's memory ceiling.

3

Pro → Team

Members and roles turn on, the management console appears, and the operators list becomes derived from the account rather than kept by hand.

4

Team → Enterprise

One role in your AWS account trusting our issuer. The key and the stores move into your account, and nothing is re-keyed by hand.

Questions

The four we are asked most

Does Free phone home?

No. Nothing runs on our side at tier 0, and there is no plan object to read. Instrumentation is opt-in, and off until you turn it on.

Can I keep my own GitHub App?

Yes, on Free and on Enterprise. In between you use the service's App and bot, which is what lets the loop receive your webhooks while your machines are asleep.

What happens when my laptop is closed?

It shows as away, with since-when, and no attention line. Its leases stand and its session clocks pause. The cloud agent keeps ticking everything else, and a takeover is offered only when a decision or approved work is waiting on that laptop.

Can I leave?

Delete the role, or delete the account. Your state, your manifest and your captures are already in your GitHub repositories in plaintext. The loop stops; the record stays yours.
