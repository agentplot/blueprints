Plans

The loop keeps turning while your laptop sleeps.

The flywheel keeps a live plan for everything you are building on GitHub. It listens to your meetings, your chat and your pull requests, turns what it hears into a plan you answer one line at a time, and runs the work you approve on your own machines or in the cloud.

The promise, in one sentence. Nothing of yours is ever in a program or on a disk that serves another company. Your code lives only on a machine created for your work and destroyed when that work ends. Everything we hold between runs is encrypted with a key that belongs to you alone, and unlocked only while your own work is running.

Moving up

Each step is one decision, not a migration

Nothing has to be set up twice, and nothing you already have stops working.

1

Free → Hobby

Sign in with the same GitHub account you already use. Your plan, your history and everyone you had invited come with you. You pick a preset, your cloud agent starts, and your laptop stays in the loop as one of your machines.

2

Hobby → Pro

Cloud build machines appear under Machines. You choose an image and a size, and the next work you approve runs there instead of waiting for your laptop.

3

Pro → Team

Invite your teammates. Roles, ownership and assignment switch on, the admin console appears, and who is on the team is simply who is in your account.

4

Team → Enterprise

Create one role in your AWS account that trusts us. Your key and your stored plan move into your account, with nothing to set up again. The same role can move the machines too, and the console then starts a cloud agent and build machines of your own.

Questions

The five we are asked most

Does Free phone home?

No. On Free, nothing of yours reaches us at all, because nothing of yours runs on our side. Usage reporting is something you switch on, and it is off until you do.

Do I need an AI key of my own?

No, on any plan. Your own key is welcome everywhere and required nowhere. Hobby needs none because it barely uses AI: every decision comes with its answers as buttons, chat answers are short commands like yes or no with the item number, and delivering the plan uses no AI at all. Anything you type in plain language on the page is handled in your browser, and new items are sorted once a day on smaller models within your included usage. Adding your own key to Hobby gets you sorting the moment items arrive and plain language in chat, both of which are included from Pro up. Go past your included usage and you get a notice and a slower pace, never a stopped loop.

Can I keep my own GitHub app?

Yes, on Free and on Enterprise. In between you use ours, which is what lets the loop hear from GitHub, Slack and Discord while your machines are asleep.

What happens when my laptop is closed?

It simply shows as away, with the time it went away, and nothing to answer. Whatever it was working on is held for it, and its clocks pause. Your cloud agent keeps everything else moving, and it offers to hand a job to another machine only when something is genuinely waiting on that laptop.

Can I leave?

Delete the role, or delete the account. Your plan, your settings and your history are already sitting in your own GitHub repositories in plain text. The loop stops. The record stays yours.
