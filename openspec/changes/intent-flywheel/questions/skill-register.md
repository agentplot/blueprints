## The question
Review objects that several sections argue rather than instruct, and asks
for forceful prose with the reasoning removed. The
mechanism-is-not-a-justification rule requires an artifact to state the
fact that makes a constraint correct. Both cannot govern the same sentence.

## What turns on it
Whether roughly 190 lines across two skills get compressed to instruction
or kept as argued rules. Answering it after the dynamic-workflow layer
lands avoids rewriting the same prose twice.

## What is already known
The two demands may not conflict as sharply as they read: a rule can state
its warrant in one clause rather than a paragraph. The measurable version
of the objection is length, and the five session-type skills at 80 to 97
lines do not draw it while the two loop skills at 366 and 429 do.
