# Question: what earns a plannotator round?

- **State:** open
- **Raised by:** the operator via dispatch, 2026-08-10: "I'm getting shown
  lots of draft decisions in plannotator that really don't seem like they
  require a review. I don't know where those are coming from."
- **Blocks:** nothing here; shapes every planning session's work order
  once closed

## The question
They come from planning-type design sessions putting every decision draft
of a batch into the round — no rule sets a threshold, so sessions default
to showing everything. The candidate criterion, carried with the
feedback: a decision that turns on operator judgment earns a round; a
draft whose answer is already forced by a prior decision or by the
evidence does not, and closes without one — recorded and reported rather
than shown. Is that the rule, and who applies it: the session drafting,
or the conductor charging the batch?

## What turns on it
Desk time is the loop's scarcest resource
(→ questions/desk-round-collision.md), and rounds padded with forced
drafts train the operator to skim — spending the desk channel's trust
exactly where the loop needs it most, now that release documents ride the
desk too.

## What is already known
The standing four-draft round predates any threshold rule; its session
showed everything, correctly by the rules of its day.
