## The question

and closes the moment an intent or bolt owns an area — so a two-line fix
inside any owned scope rides proposal-spec-review-build ceremony. Two
pressure valves exist and neither reaches the felt problem: the bolt
conductor's three direct edits (a closed list by decision), and
`bolt-quick` (no scheduled review, but the full change shape). What is
the floor — a small-work lane inside owned scopes, a wider direct-edit
list, a lighter shape a conductor can grant per task — and what boundary
keeps it from reopening the untracked-edit hole the loop exists to close?

correctly and the operator still experiences the sum as crap. The chore
route's closure is deliberate and was earned — six machinery tasks
misfiled under Writeback by a conductor that had read the rules — so the
answer must hold both truths at once.
